01 · My Story

About Me

I'm an engineering student at the Georgia Institute of Technology with a background in applied mathematics and computer science from Emory University. My academic journey has strengthened my analytical problem-solving skills, while Georgia Tech has given me opportunities to apply them through hands-on engineering projects.

During my mandatory military service in South Korea, I served at the Joint Security Area (JSA) along the Korean Demilitarized Zone. I witnessed fellow soldiers experience serious back injuries from physically demanding tasks, sometimes severe enough to end their service. That experience inspired my interest in wearable robotics and exoskeletons that can support human movement and reduce the risk of injury.

I'm developing skills in computer-aided design (CAD), electronics, and mechanical system design. Beyond engineering, I enjoy playing guitar as a proud member of GT Oscar Band. I hope to combine my academic background and personal experiences to develop practical technologies that improve safety and mobility.

03 · Looking Ahead

Career Goals

My long-term goal is to develop engineering technologies that improve human mobility, prevent injuries, and support individuals working in physically demanding environments. I hope to achieve this by strengthening my technical skills, participating in wearable robotics research at Georgia Tech, and pursuing opportunities to serve military personnel and veterans. Through these experiences, I aim to combine engineering innovation with a commitment to public service.

Build Technical Skills

Continue developing my skills in CAD, programming, circuit analysis, and prototyping through coursework, hands-on projects, and independent practice. I aim to strengthen my ability to design, analyze, and build functional engineering systems while gaining experience integrating mechanical, electrical, and software components. These skills will provide a strong foundation for pursuing a career in robotics.

Gain Project/Research Experience

Pursue research opportunities at Georgia Tech's EPIC Lab to help design, prototype, and test wearable robotic systems that augment human movement and reduce the risk of injury. Through collaborative research, I hope to gain hands-on experience with the engineering design process, from developing initial concepts to evaluating and improving prototypes. I also aim to learn from experienced researchers and contribute to projects that address real-world challenges.

Serve the Veteran Community

Explore opportunities to serve as a combat medic in the Georgia Army National Guard while continuing to support U.S. and South Korean veteran communities. I hope to combine my military experience, medical training, and engineering background to improve the health, safety, and mobility of service members and veterans.
